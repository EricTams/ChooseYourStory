# Razzy — Act Two script

Illustrated draft through the end of Act Two. The reader starts at `meet_razzy`, the beginning of Act One. Act Three begins with Razzy acting to defend the school; it is not implemented yet.

## Continuity

- The notebook replaces the loose-map discovery. Plans are tucked into its back cover; Razzy takes them out for navigation while the notebook travels in his backpack.
- Razzy becomes absorbed in reading while the expedition departs. Hours pass before he heads upstairs; he emerges in late afternoon. His curiosity both delays him and teaches him about the school.
- Departure remains an innocent seating-list mix-up, aided by the weasel distracting Burrowes while the ferret checks the gate. The burglars do not know Razzy is inside.
- Spectacles discovery removed from the playable graph. Old art remains archived; no branch carries that clue. Burrowes’s eventual exposure needs other evidence.
- Route: shaft landing → short passage/chamber → old passage junction → service stairs → kitchen cellar → kitchen → courtyard. The notebook map is illustrative, not a precise navigation puzzle.
- Fresh excavation remains unexplained to Razzy until the burglars mention their tunnel under the garden wall. Its exact connection to the vault and the drain needs a simple consistent layout before Act Three trap art.
- Burglars go to fetch tools, giving Razzy a short interval to act. Act Two ends at his realization; no trap starts here.

## B01: Beneath the School

The loose earth breaks Razzy’s fall.
“Hello? Can anyone hear me?”
Nobody answers. He tries to steady his breathing.
The wall is crumbly, but he might be able to climb it. Or he could try the low passage. He can see a little way inside.

**Picture:** Razzy sits safely on the loose sandy bank beneath the shaft, shaken and dusty, calling up toward the light. His backpack remains on his shoulders. A single low arched passage opens at right.

For a moment, his claws hold. Then the mortar gives way.
“No, no—!”
Razzy slides back down. He spits out a little dirt and looks up.
He won’t get out that way.

Razzy ducks through. The passage is short enough for the light from the shaft to reach its other end.
“There must be another way upstairs,” he tells himself.
Then he sees the room beyond.

“Professor Burrowes was right!”

An old notebook waits in the wall niche. Perhaps its owner knew another way upstairs.

The founder wrote this! There are drawings of the school being built, with plans folded inside the back cover.
Razzy finds a passage leading to the kitchen cellar. He can get out!
Then a sketch catches his eye.
“Is that how they built the gate?”

**Picture:** Razzy studies the open founder notebook on the desk. Its folded school plan spreads from the back cover onto the tabletop. The wall niche is empty.

## B05: The Other Carriage

“Equipment group, you’re with me!” calls the badger.
“Excuse me, Professor. Where shall I leave a delivery?” asks the white weasel.
Burrowes turns to help. Hearing the badger, he moves Razzy’s name to the other carriage’s list. But the badger’s copy still has Razzy riding with Burrowes.
Both teachers finish their lists. Everyone seems ready.
The carriages leave.

**Picture:** The white weasel presents a delivery slip to Burrowes beside the first coach, drawing his attention away from boarding. Behind them the badger carries equipment to the other coach. Razzy is absent.

## B06: One More Page

Razzy reads about a tower that leaned, a gate lifted by weights, and drains running beneath the classrooms.
He keeps turning the pages. He doesn’t notice the hours passing.
Then he remembers the expedition.
“Oh! I’d better get back!”

**Picture:** Razzy is absorbed in illustrated notebook pages at the desk, a paw turning the page. The plans are folded inside. Warm light has shifted later in the day.

## B07: Not on the Plans

The steps on the plan lead toward daylight.
Then Razzy hears a dry rustling from the freshly dug tunnel. It isn’t on the drawing.
“Is someone in there?”
He wants to find the expedition—but perhaps whoever is digging can help him.

**Picture:** Razzy stands between the left stone stairs and the right earth tunnel, holding the folded plans and looking curiously toward the excavation. Neither route has been chosen. Daylight reaches him from the stairs. The right tunnel is a ragged earthen hole with exposed roots, shovel marks, loose spoil and one rough timber support, continuing into darkness instead of ending at masonry. A muddy spade leans beneath the clay drain, blade on the ground.

- Crawl into the tunnel and investigate. → `why_snakes`

## B08: Up Toward Daylight

The steps are solid beneath his paws. Razzy lets out a long breath.
“Hello? I’m coming up!”
No answer. He climbs faster. He has so much to tell everyone.

## B09: They Left Without Him

The passage brings him into the kitchen cellar. Nobody answers in the kitchen, so he runs to the courtyard.
The carriages are gone. So is everyone else.
“They left me?”
Razzy’s throat tightens. He wanted to show them what he found. Had nobody noticed he was missing?

**Picture:** Razzy stands alone beneath the familiar noticeboard, shoulders slumped. The courtyard is empty of pupils, luggage and carriages in late afternoon light.

- Listen. There are voices at the gate. → `three_days_to_steal`

## B10: Three Days

Razzy recognizes the peddlers and stays behind the gatepost.
“Every carriage gone,” says the ferret. “Your delivery trick worked.”
“Kept them looking at me while you checked the gate,” says the weasel. “Now we have three days to empty the vault.”
“Through the tunnel?”
“Under the garden wall. Fetch the tools. We’ll be inside before dark.”

**Picture:** Razzy listens concealed behind the inside gate pillar. Outside, the weasel gloats to the grinning ferret holding an empty sack. They have not entered the school.

- Stay hidden. → `alone_with_the_secret`

## B11: Nobody Else Knows

They’re going to rob the school.
Razzy holds his breath until their footsteps fade. He wishes Burrowes were here. Or the cook. Anyone.
But the teachers are miles away, and the burglars will be back with their tools.
He knows where the tunnel is. And they don’t know he’s here.

**Picture:** Razzy has withdrawn behind the ivy-covered gate pillar, frightened and thinking. No defensive action has begun; the burglars are out of view.

End of Act Two.
